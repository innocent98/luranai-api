WEYSURE INSURANCE - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

About Weysure Insurance
========================

Weysure Insurance is a leading provider of comprehensive vehicle and property insurance solutions. We offer competitive rates, 24/7 customer support, and fast claim processing to give you peace of mind.


Vehicle Insurance Coverage
==========================

What does my auto insurance cover?
Our standard auto insurance policy includes:
- Collision coverage for damage to your vehicle
- Liability coverage up to $500,000 for bodily injury and property damage
- Comprehensive coverage for theft, vandalism, and natural disasters
- Emergency roadside assistance included at no extra cost
- Rental car reimbursement up to $50/day for 30 days

What is NOT covered?
Standard policies do not cover:
- Damage from racing or intentional acts
- Wear and tear or mechanical breakdown
- Personal belongings stolen from your vehicle (covered under homeowners insurance)


Claims Process
==============

How do I file a claim?
You can file a claim 24/7 through:
1. Online at weysure.com/claims
2. Mobile app (Weysure Go)
3. Phone: 1-800-WEYSURE (1-800-939-7873)
4. Email: claims@weysure.com

What information do I need?
- Policy number
- Date, time, and location of incident
- Description of what happened
- Photos of damage (if possible)
- Police report number (for accidents)
- Contact information for other parties involved

How long does claim processing take?
- Initial review: 24-48 hours
- Simple claims: 3-5 business days
- Complex claims: 10-15 business days
- You'll receive updates via email and SMS throughout the process


Payments and Billing
=====================

When is my payment due?
Premium payments are due on the 1st of each month. You can set up:
- Auto-pay for automatic monthly deductions
- Quarterly or annual payment plans (5% discount on annual)
- One-time payments via credit card, debit card, or bank transfer

What happens if I miss a payment?
- Grace period: 10 days from due date
- Late fee: $25 after grace period
- Policy suspension: 15 days past due
- Policy cancellation: 30 days past due
We'll send reminders via email and SMS before any action is taken.


Policy Changes
==============

Can I add a driver to my policy?
Yes! You can add drivers anytime:
- Spouse/domestic partner: No additional fee
- Teen driver (16-19): Additional premium applies
- Occasional driver (uses car less than 25% of time): Small fee

All drivers must pass our verification process, which includes:
- Valid driver's license check
- Driving record review
- May affect your premium based on driving history

Can I change my coverage?
You can increase or decrease coverage at any time:
- Changes take effect within 24 hours
- Premium adjustments are prorated
- Decreasing coverage may require a safety inspection
