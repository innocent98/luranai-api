Subscription Plans and Limits (Tenant Version)

Current tiers
- trial
- lite
- professional
- growth
- enterprise

Current public pricing references
- Lite: ₦9,000/month
- Professional: ₦40,000/month
- Growth: ₦80,000/month
- Enterprise: custom pricing (contact sales)

Trial basics
- Trial starts at signup.
- Trial duration is currently 30 days.
- Trial defaults include:
  - 200 conversations/month
  - 2 knowledge sources
  - 10 MB knowledge storage
  - 1 team member
  - 0 flows
  - no URL knowledge sources

Starter capability examples by tier

Lite
- 2,000 conversations/month
- 15 knowledge sources
- 50 MB storage
- 3 team members
- 2 flows
- URL sources enabled
- webhooks not enabled

Professional
- 10,000 conversations/month
- 50 knowledge sources
- 200 MB storage
- 10 team members
- 10 flows
- webhooks enabled (up to 3 per flow)

Growth
- 25,000 conversations/month
- 50 knowledge sources
- 500 MB storage
- 20 team members
- 25 flows
- webhooks enabled (up to 10 per flow)

Enterprise
- conversation, storage, and flow limits are custom/unlimited by plan config
- webhooks and team limits are custom

How limits are enforced
- Limits are checked at runtime. If your plan limit is reached, action is blocked.
- Common enforced limits:
  - monthly conversation quota
  - knowledge source count
  - knowledge storage
  - team member count
  - flow count
  - webhook usage (by tier)

What happens when subscription is inactive
- If trial expires or subscription becomes inactive, protected features are blocked until reactivation.

Note
- Plan details can be updated by platform policy. Always confirm latest values on your in-app Pricing page.
