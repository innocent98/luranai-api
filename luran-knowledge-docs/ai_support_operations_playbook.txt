Luran AI Support Operations Playbook (Tenant Version)

Purpose
This guide explains how to run a high-quality support assistant in Luran using only verified product behavior. It is written for support leaders, tenant admins, and day-to-day operators.

What your assistant should do
Your Luran assistant is designed to support your customers about your business, services, and support workflows.
It should:
- answer in-scope customer support questions
- ask clarifying questions when details are missing
- avoid guessing when information is uncertain
- escalate to a human when needed
- stay professional and calm, even if the customer is upset

How AI behavior is controlled in Settings → AI Configuration
You can shape assistant behavior using these controls:

1) Brand Voice
- Tone: professional, friendly, or casual
- Formality: formal, balanced, or casual
- Greeting style: warm, neutral, or enthusiastic

2) Knowledge Grounding
- Grounding requirement: controls whether the assistant must stay closely tied to your available knowledge
- Grounding mode:
  - strict: strongest protection against unsupported claims
  - balanced: practical default for most teams
  - flexible: allows broader handling while still following tenant rules

3) Policy behavior when confidence is low or no clear knowledge is found
For both of these situations, you can choose one action:
- answer: provide a safe in-scope response
- clarify: ask one focused follow-up question
- escalate: offer human support handoff
- refuse: politely decline and redirect to relevant support scope

4) Topic Controls
- Allowed Topics: topics the assistant is expected to focus on
- Blocked Topics: topics the assistant should decline
- Escalation Keywords: words/phrases that trigger faster human handoff behavior

5) Custom Instructions
- You can add tenant-specific instructions to guide style, scope, and policy behavior
- Maximum length is 500 characters
- Best use: concise, high-priority rules only

Current response style policy
Luran runtime instructions currently enforce concise, professional responses and no emoji output.

How to write strong Custom Instructions (recommended pattern)
Keep Custom Instructions short and operational.
Use this structure:
- Scope: what your assistant should help with
- Boundaries: what it must not do
- Evidence rule: how to respond when information is missing
- Escalation rule: when to hand off
- Style rule: response length and tone

Example:
"Support customers about account, billing, onboarding, and product usage for this company. Do not guess policy or pricing details. If missing info, ask one clarifying question, then offer escalation. Keep replies professional and concise."

Knowledge Base operations that directly affect answer quality
1) Source types currently supported
- text files
- markdown files
- PDF files
- DOCX files
- webpage URL sources (plan-dependent)

2) File size and quality checks
- Max file size per uploaded file: 10 MB
- Empty files are rejected
- Files without meaningful text are rejected

3) Source lifecycle
- PROCESSING: uploaded and being prepared
- ACTIVE: ready for assistant use
- FAILED: could not be prepared
- INACTIVE: removed from active use

4) Practical quality rule
If your most important sources are not ACTIVE, expect weaker answers.

What to do when customers ask out-of-scope requests
Your assistant should politely decline unrelated requests and redirect the customer back to supported help.
Examples of out-of-scope requests include personal writing tasks unrelated to your company support.

Support escalation and human handoff workflow
Luran supports both AI and human assistance in one journey.
A typical path is:
1. customer asks question
2. AI tries to resolve
3. if needed, case is escalated
4. support team continues from the dashboard with context

Ticket operations available to support teams
Support tickets can be:
- created
- assigned
- moved into progress
- resolved
- closed

Priority levels used in tickets:
- low
- medium
- high
- urgent

Team roles and responsibilities
Tenant roles are:
- Tenant Admin: full configuration and team control
- Support Agent: handles support conversations and tickets
- Viewer: read-only visibility

Important team rule
Only Tenant Admins can invite/manage team members and change key tenant settings.

Customer feedback and service quality tracking
Luran supports customer session ratings and ratings analytics.
Use this to improve:
- resolution quality
- escalation quality
- support content gaps

Simple monthly quality routine
1. Review low-rated conversations
2. identify repeated customer questions
3. add/refresh knowledge for those questions
4. add or refine guided flows for repetitive journeys
5. tune AI policy actions (answer/clarify/escalate/refuse)
6. retest with real customer-like prompts

Subscription and limits (operator guidance)
Your workspace behavior is affected by plan capabilities, including:
- monthly conversation allowance
- knowledge storage allowance
- number of knowledge sources
- team member allowance
- flow allowance

If a limit is reached, related actions are blocked until usage is reduced or plan is adjusted.

Trial expectations (default onboarding behavior)
New trial workspaces start with a limited setup suitable for evaluation, including restricted knowledge, team, and flow capacity compared with paid tiers.

Safe go-live checklist
Before launch:
1. Verify top support documents are ACTIVE
2. Set AI Configuration (grounding mode, policy actions, topic controls)
3. Add clear Custom Instructions (within 500 chars)
4. Confirm escalation path works end-to-end
5. Confirm support team roles and ownership
6. Run test conversations for:
   - a standard in-scope question
   - a missing-information question
   - an out-of-scope request
   - an escalation-required case

Ongoing maintenance checklist
Weekly:
- review unresolved or escalated conversations
- update missing knowledge topics
- remove outdated policy content

Monthly:
- review ratings trends
- refine AI rules and support playbooks
- validate subscription limits against current support volume

Final operating principle
Best results come from alignment between three things:
- clear support scope
- current and accurate knowledge
- consistent escalation handling

When these stay aligned, the assistant remains useful, trustworthy, and professional for your customers.
