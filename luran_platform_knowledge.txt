ABOUT LURAN
================================================================================

What is Luran?
Luran is an AI-powered customer support platform that enables businesses to deploy intelligent chatbots on their websites. Our platform uses advanced natural language processing and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to provide accurate, context-aware responses to customer inquiries 24/7.

What makes Luran unique?
- Knowledge-grounded responses: AI answers are based on your actual business documentation
- Smart escalation: Automatically escalates complex queries to human agents
- Multi-tenant architecture: Secure, isolated environments for each company
- Customizable AI behavior: Configure brand voice, topics, and response style
- Real-time agent handoff: Seamless transition from AI to human support
- Comprehensive analytics: Track performance, satisfaction, and knowledge gaps

Who is Luran for?
Luran is ideal for:
- Insurance companies managing policy inquiries
- E-commerce businesses handling product questions
- SaaS companies providing technical support
- Service providers managing appointment bookings
- Any business with repetitive customer inquiries

SECURITY & DATA PROTECTION
================================================================================

How does Luran protect my data?
Luran implements multiple layers of security to protect your business data and customer conversations:

Encryption
- Data in Transit: All data transmitted between your systems and Luran is encrypted using TLS 1.3
- Data at Rest: All stored data (conversations, documents, customer information) is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
- Database Encryption: PostgreSQL databases use transparent data encryption
- End-to-End Security: From widget to database, your data remains encrypted

Access Control
- Multi-Tenant Isolation: Complete data separation between companies - you can only access your own data
- Role-Based Access: Granular permissions (Tenant Admin, Support Agent, Viewer) control who can see and do what
- Authentication: Industry-standard JWT tokens with configurable expiration
- API Key Security: Separate public and secret keys with proper scoping
- Password Security: Bcrypt hashing with salt for all passwords
- Session Management: Automatic session timeout and revocation capabilities

Infrastructure Security
- Regular Security Audits: Third-party penetration testing and vulnerability assessments
- Automated Vulnerability Scanning: Continuous monitoring for security issues
- Secure Infrastructure: Hosted in SOC 2 compliant data centers
- DDoS Protection: CloudFlare protection against distributed denial of service attacks
- Firewall Protection: Network-level security with strict ingress/egress rules
- Intrusion Detection: Real-time monitoring for suspicious activity

KNOWLEDGE BASE MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

What is a knowledge base?
Your knowledge base is the collection of documents, FAQs, and information that powers your AI chatbot. The AI uses this knowledge to answer customer questions accurately and consistently.

What types of documents can I upload?
Supported formats:
- Plain text (.txt)
- Markdown (.md)
- PDF documents (.pdf)
- Microsoft Word (.docx)
- Maximum file size: 10MB per document

Best practices:
- Use clear, structured content with headings
- Keep documents focused on specific topics
- Update regularly to maintain accuracy
- Remove outdated information promptly

How do I upload knowledge documents?
1. Navigate to Knowledge Management in your dashboard
2. Click "Upload Knowledge"
3. Choose your file or paste text directly
4. Select document type (FAQ, Help Doc, Policy, or SOP)
5. Add optional metadata (category, author, language)
6. Click "Upload and Process"

Processing typically takes 30 seconds to 2 minutes depending on document size.

What happens during document processing?
Our system:
1. Extracts text from your document
2. Breaks it into semantic chunks (meaningful sections)
3. Generates embeddings (mathematical representations)
4. Stores chunks in our vector database
5. Indexes for fast retrieval during conversations

This enables the AI to quickly find relevant information when answering questions.

How do I update existing knowledge?
You can:
- Upload a new version of a document (automatically creates v2, v3, etc.)
- Edit individual chunks through the knowledge detail view
- Delete outdated sources entirely
- Mark sources as active/inactive

Previous versions are preserved for audit purposes.

How many documents can I upload?
There is no hard limit on the number of documents. However, we recommend:
- Keep individual documents under 50 pages
- Focus on quality over quantity
- Organize by topic for easier management
- Remove duplicate or redundant information

AI CHATBOT CONFIGURATION
================================================================================

How do I customize my chatbot's personality?
Navigate to Settings > AI Configuration to adjust:

Brand Voice:
- Tone: Professional, friendly, casual, or formal
- Formality level: Very formal, balanced, or casual
- Greeting style: Warm, professional, or brief
- Emoji usage: Enable or disable

These settings ensure your AI matches your brand identity.

What are allowed and blocked topics?
Allowed topics: Define what your chatbot should help with
- Examples: "billing questions", "product information", "technical support"
- The AI focuses on these areas and stays on-brand

Blocked topics: Prevent the AI from discussing certain subjects
- Examples: "medical advice", "legal advice", "competitor products"
- The AI politely declines and may escalate to human agents

How do escalation keywords work?
Escalation keywords trigger automatic handoff to human agents when detected:
- Default examples: "speak to manager", "file complaint", "cancel account"
- Customize based on your business needs
- Case-insensitive matching
- Can trigger immediate escalation or offer escalation to the customer

What is the confidence threshold?
The confidence threshold (default: 0.55) determines when the AI should answer vs. escalate:
- Higher threshold (0.7-0.8): More conservative, fewer but more accurate answers
- Lower threshold (0.4-0.5): More willing to answer, may occasionally be less precise
- Recommended: 0.55-0.65 for most businesses

The AI checks if it has sufficient knowledge before responding.

Can I set maximum response length?
No! max_response_tokens has default length per tier

What are custom instructions?
Custom instructions let you add specific guidance for your AI, such as:
- "Always mention our 24/7 support hotline for urgent issues"
- "Be empathetic when discussing billing problems"
- "Suggest related products when appropriate"
- "Use metric units for international customers"

What happens when a conversation is escalated?
When the AI escalates to a human agent:

3. Assigned agents receive notifications
4. Customer sees "Connecting you with an agent..."

3. Click "Take Over" to join the conversation

What is a conversation session?
A session represents one continuous conversation between a customer and your AI:
- Starts when customer opens the chat widget
- Continues across multiple messages
- Includes full message history
- Can be escalated to human agents
- Ends when customer closes chat or after inactivity

Sessions help track conversation flow and resolution.

SUPPORT TICKETS & AGENT DASHBOARD
================================================================================

What is a support ticket?
A support ticket is created when:
- AI escalates a conversation automatically
- Agent manually escalates an active conversation
- Agent creates a ticket manually from the dashboard

Tickets track issues through to resolution.

What ticket statuses are available?
Ticket lifecycle:
- Open: Newly created, awaiting assignment
- In Progress: Agent is actively working on it
- Resolved: Issue has been fixed (requires resolution notes)
- Closed: Completed and archived (requires ticket to be resolved first)

Tickets must be resolved before they can be closed.

How do I assign tickets to agents?
Tenant admins can:
1. View unassigned tickets in the dashboard
2. Click "Assign" on any ticket
3. Select an agent from your team
4. Agent receives notification immediately

Agents can also self-assign tickets they want to handle.

What ticket priorities are available?
- Low: General inquiries, non-urgent requests
- Medium: Standard customer issues
- High: Issues affecting customer experience
- Urgent: Critical problems requiring immediate attention

Priority is assigned automatically based on conversation context and keywords.

What are ticket numbers?
Every ticket gets a unique number in format TKT-YYYY-NNNN:
- TKT-2026-0001: First ticket of 2026
- TKT-2026-0142: 142nd ticket of 2026

Ticket numbers help with tracking and customer reference.

Can agents work on multiple tickets simultaneously?
Yes! Agents can:
- View all assigned tickets in their queue
- Switch between active conversations
- Prioritize based on urgency
- Use WebSocket real-time chat for instant responses
